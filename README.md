STATEMENT OF PURPOSE  

 

My interest in computer science began not in a classroom, but while working on an RFID-based EV charging system during my final year of Electronics and Communication Engineering. The project involved developing an embedded system to authenticate users and manage charging sessions. Seeing how software could control physical hardware reliably sparked my interest in software development and motivated me to pursue a career in the field. It was the experience that made me realise I wanted to go beyond working with electronic circuits and explore the broader possibilities of software and computing. 
 
My B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Visvesvaraya Technological University provided me with a foundation in digital system design, microcontrollers, embedded systems, and Verilog HDL. Alongside my core curriculum, I completed C programming as an additional module, which strengthened my understanding of programming fundamentals. My academic coursework also included operating systems and computer networks, giving me an understanding of how software interacts with hardware, manages system resources, and communicates across networks. These subjects provided a foundation relevant to software development and encouraged me to explore computer science beyond my undergraduate specialization. Recognizing the importance of deeper knowledge in this field, I continued building my understanding through independent learning, academic projects, and professional experience. 

Recognizing the need to strengthen my knowledge of computer science, I actively worked to bridge the gap between my electronics background and software development. During my internship, I developed a car information management platform, which gave me practical exposure to application development. Alongside my academic and professional learning, I completed courses in Machine Learning and Data Science through Coursera and AWS through Udemy. I also strengthened my understanding of data structures and problem-solving through independent practice on GeeksforGeeks. These learning experiences helped me build on my programming foundation, explore emerging technologies, and develop a broader understanding of modern software applications. 

For the past three years, I have been working as a Software Engineer at First American India on AttorneyFirst, a title insurance platform that supports the home-closing process in Chicago. The platform connects attorneys, examiners, and homeowners through a unified system. During my time there, I have contributed to several initiatives involving document automation, software testing, and cloud infrastructure. One of my key contributions was implementing and integrating an Azure AI Document Intelligence model into the document-processing workflow. Using sample documents stored on AWS EC2, I helped automate information extraction, reducing the order creation process from five to ten minutes of manual work to under 40 seconds. This significantly improved processing efficiency and reduced manual effort. 

I contributed as well to an AWS infrastructure migration that improved security by removing direct database access and placing services behind CloudFront. I helped review the architecture for unused resources and gaps in the deployment setup. I also helped introduce error tracing across the user interface and API layers. These projects taught me how design decisions affect the reliability, security, and maintainability of software after it is released.			 
 
This programme appeals to me because it would let me build on my electronics background and software engineering experience while strengthening my foundation in computer science. Through my work on document automation, testing, and cloud infrastructure, I have seen how software design decisions affect the reliability and scalability of production systems. I now want to understand those decisions more deeply and develop the knowledge to design complex software systems. The programme would help me connect what I have learned in practice with the broader computer science knowledge I need to grow toward software architecture.	 
 
Outside my technical work, I have taken on leadership and team responsibilities. At university, I led the State Robothon, where colleges competed in robotics challenges, and helped organize an Arduino workshop for my classmates. At work, I have contributed to employee engagement activities and received a quarterly award for my contributions to OCR, infrastructure migration, and the shift-left initiative. 

I am applying for a master's degree because I want to build on my engineering background and professional experience with deeper knowledge of computer science and software engineering. In the short term, I hope to work on cloud-based and AI-enabled systems. Over time, I would like to take on software architecture responsibilities, helping teams design reliable systems and make sound technical decisions. 

I would bring practical experience, a strong interest in how software systems are designed, and a clear reason for returning to formal study. I look forward to developing the knowledge I need to take on broader engineering responsibilities.
